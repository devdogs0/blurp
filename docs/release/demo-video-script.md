# Demo video script (about 30 seconds)

Capture on the Galaxy Z Fold8, inner screen, then cover. No Apple names, no other apps’ private content if you can avoid it.

1. **0–5 s** — Open Blurp on a fresh install. Show the disclosure: checkbox, Continue.
2. **5–12 s** — Open Settings (Installed apps → Blurp) and flip the switch on. Return. Tutorial: “Close your phone, then open it.”
3. **12–20 s** — Home screen visible. Fold about halfway; the swinging half frosts. Open again; frost clears.
4. **20–26 s** — Lock the phone, wake the lock screen, fold; frost over the clock. Open; lock screen sharp again.
5. **26–30 s** — Open Blurp; Fold frost switch on. Done.

If Play only wants the accessibility use: cut 1, 2, and 5 together with one fold.
