# Store listing

**Title (≤ 50):** Blurp: Fold Frost

**Short description (≤ 80):**
Frost the swinging half of your Fold while you open and close it.

**Full description:**

Blurp frosts the half of the screen that’s moving when you fold or unfold your phone — on the home screen, in other apps, and on the lock screen. You don’t have to keep the app open.

Made for Samsung Galaxy Z Fold7, Z Fold8 and Z Fold8 Ultra.

After a short one-time setup (Blurp opens Accessibility, where you turn it on under Installed apps), just close your phone, then open it.

You can switch the frost off in the app at any time. Blurp does not read what is on your screen.

Free, with no ads and no internet access. Blurp is open source under the GPL-3.0, so anyone can check what it does.
