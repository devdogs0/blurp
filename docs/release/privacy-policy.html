Blurp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy is for the Android app Blurp (package ai.devdogs.blurp).

Summary

Blurp collects nothing. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What Blurp does

Blurp frosts the swinging half of the screen on a Samsung Galaxy Z Fold7, Z Fold8 or Z Fold8 Ultra while you fold and unfold the phone. It does this by drawing a blur overlay. It is not an accessibility tool for people with disabilities. It uses Android’s Accessibility service only so that overlay can appear over the home screen, other apps, and the lock screen.

Accessibility service

Blurp’s Accessibility service does not read, collect, store, or share anything on your screen. It does not look at window content. It only draws a blur on top. You can turn the service off in Settings at any time. You can also turn the frost off inside the app; the service then stays on but draws nothing.

Network access

Blurp does not request Android’s internet permission and does not use the advertising ID. Its source code is public, so anyone can check this.

What Blurp stores on the device

Only on this phone, Blurp remembers whether you agreed to the Accessibility disclosure, whether you finished the tutorial, and whether the frost switch is on. Uninstalling Blurp deletes them.

Children

Blurp is not directed at children under 13.
